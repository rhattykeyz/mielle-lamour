# Mielle L'Amour — how to use the admin panel

This is for the salon owner. You do not need to change any code. Use a laptop or a phone.

Open **yoursite.com/admin** and sign in with the email and password you were given.

## Every day

1. **Dashboard** — who is booked today, the next week, and any bookings still waiting for a deposit.
2. **Calendar** — tap a booking to mark it confirmed, completed, cancelled or no-show. For bridal/group bookings, tap **Mark deposit received** when Mobile Money arrives.
3. **New booking** — use this for walk-ins and people who book by phone or WhatsApp, so the slot is blocked for online clients too.

## Changing the menu (nails, spa, new treatments)

Go to **Services**.

- Add a **category** (for example “Lashes”) whenever you offer a new type of work. The website homepage and services page update on their own.
- Add a **service** with duration, price, and whether the price is “from”.
- Tick **Requires deposit** for bridal, groups or long sessions. Set an amount in GHS or a percent.
- **Hide** anything you are not offering right now instead of deleting it, if you might bring it back.

## Team

**Staff** — add a person, upload a photo, tick which services they do, set working hours for each weekday, and add holiday dates. If they do not offer a service, they will not appear for that service when clients book.

## Photos and words

- **Gallery** — choose a category (Nails, Beauty, Spa, or add your own), then select many phone photos at once. They are compressed so the website stays fast. Use Up/Down to change the order.
- **Content** — edit the homepage and about-page text, and add real reviews when you have them. Delete or hide the sample reviews.

## Business details

**Settings** is where you put the real:

- Address  
- Opening hours  
- Phone and WhatsApp (leave blank until you decide — the site will not show broken buttons)  
- Email, Instagram, TikTok  
- Google Maps embed  
- How far ahead people can book, how much notice you need, buffer time between clients  
- Cancellation policy and privacy notice  
- Mobile Money name, network and number for deposits  
- Logo file (circular gold and baby-pink badge)

**Holidays & closures** — add public holidays or fit-out days so nobody can book them.

## Clients and Excel

**Clients** — search by name or phone. See their history and how many no-shows they have.

**Export** — pick dates and download an Excel file of bookings (and a separate clients file). Phone numbers stay as text so Excel does not drop the starting zero.

## If something looks wrong

- A time still showing as busy after a cancel: refresh the calendar. Cancelled bookings free the slot.
- A client says they did not get email: check they typed an email, then ask your developer to confirm Resend is connected.
- Forgotten password: use “reset password” if it was set up on your email, or ask the person who built the site to send a recovery email from Supabase.
